Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about Nobelova Gradani Academy programmes, delivery options and booking.

What does Nobelova Gradani Academy offer?

The Academy offers professional certificates, diplomas and practical training in mental health, developmental surveillance, early intervention, language disorders, neurodevelopmental disorders and data analysis.

Who are the programmes for?

Programmes are designed for educators, school teams, clinicians, caregivers, practitioners, organisations and anyone building practical skills in child development and related support services.

Are the courses available online?

Training can be discussed for delivery in Lagos, virtually or through an organisation-based booking. Contact the Academy to confirm the next available format.

How do I book training?

Complete the training booking form, choose a programme and preferred start date, then submit your request. The Nobelova Gradani team will follow up by phone, email or WhatsApp.
